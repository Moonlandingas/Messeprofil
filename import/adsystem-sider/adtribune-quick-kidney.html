A promotional counter in a new shape that sets up in seconds

adTribune Quick Kidney expands the Quick family with a form inspired by the shape of a kidney. The counter catches the eye with its soft, flowing line and gives you greater freedom when arranging your booth space. Its structure is based on a pneumatic mechanism — simply lift the tabletop and it automatically locks into the correct position. The entire process takes just a moment, allowing you to focus on preparing your event rather than assembling equipment. It’s a solution designed for trade shows, events and promotional campaigns.

Advantages
Instant setup
The pneumatic system ensures quick preparation of the counter. Simply lift the tabletop and the frame unfolds automatically and locks securely into place. The graphic is installed by inserting a silicone edge into a groove along the tabletop edge, ensuring the fabric stretches evenly across the structure and looks smooth and professional — without wrinkles or adjustments.
Distinctive shape
The characteristic rounded line makes the counter stand out from classic, straight forms. It works perfectly in networking areas — allowing easy access from different sides and encouraging natural conversations. The counter can become the central point of a small meeting zone or complement a larger booth.
Durable print and flexible fabric
We use dye-sublimation printing to ensure intense colors and sharp details. The dye penetrates directly into the fabric structure, preventing cracking and ensuring long-lasting graphic quality.

One structure, many uses

adTribune Quick Kidney can easily be adapted to the nature of the event and your brand identity. It can serve as a registration desk at a conference, an information point at a trade show, a product presentation station, or a space for individual client meetings. Depending on the graphic design, it can shift in character — from formal and business-oriented to more casual and event-focused.

You can order the print separately, replace it before your next event, or choose a complete set or the structure alone. This means one counter offers multiple arrangement possibilities and allows you to respond flexibly to the needs of different events — without investing in entirely new solutions.
