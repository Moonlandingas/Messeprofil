A round promotional counter you can set up in seconds

adTribune Quick Round is another version of the Quick family — this time in a classic round form. The symmetrical shape makes it ideal as a central booth feature and naturally encourages access from every side. The pneumatic mechanism known from previous models allows it to be ready in moments — just lift the tabletop and the structure automatically locks into position. It’s a practical solution for trade shows, events and promotional campaigns.

Advantages
Instant setup
The pneumatic system ensures quick preparation of the counter. Simply lift the tabletop and the frame unfolds automatically and locks securely into place. The graphic is installed by inserting a silicone edge into a groove along the tabletop edge, ensuring the fabric stretches evenly across the structure and looks smooth and professional — without wrinkles or adjustments.
Distinctive shape
The characteristic rounded line makes the counter stand out from classic, straight forms. It works perfectly in networking areas — allowing easy access from different sides and encouraging natural conversations. The counter can become the central point of a small meeting zone or complement a larger booth.
Durable print and flexible fabric
We use dye-sublimation printing to ensure intense colors and sharp details. The dye penetrates directly into the fabric structure, preventing cracking and ensuring long-lasting graphic quality.

Consistent form, many possibilities

adTribune Quick Round can easily be adapted to the character of any event. It can serve as a registration desk, information point, product presentation area or client meeting space. Changing the graphic quickly gives it a new look and allows you to adapt communication to a specific campaign.

The model is available as a complete set, graphic only, or structure only, allowing flexible management of your event equipment and use of one reliable structure in multiple variations.
