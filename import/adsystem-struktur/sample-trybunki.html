LED Advertising Counters

With effective LED backlighting, your booth will attract attention from a distance, encouraging attendees to engage during trade shows, conferences, or marketing events.

LED counters are easy to assemble and disassemble, offering convenience and mobility that allows you to quickly move from one event to another. Additionally, replaceable graphics let you update your promotional content as needed, adapting to changing market trends and campaign objectives.

Advertising counters for trade shows and promotions

Exhibition counters provide a professional space for showcasing products, engaging with customers, and building business relationships. It is crucial for the counter to be suited to the nature of the event—whether it is a trade show, conference, or smaller exhibition. With customization options, each product can be adapted to the current campaign, allowing for a cohesive and attractive brand presentation. Furthermore, the intuitive setup makes preparing the entire booth quick and hassle-free. By choosing the right counter, you ensure that your booth is professional, and potential clients won't pass by unnoticed.

Trade show counter – how to choose the best model?

The choice of a counter should be based on the nature of the event and the company's marketing objectives. It is essential for the trade show counter to be stable, easy to set up, and offer the option to change the graphics. This last feature allows the product to be adapted to different promotional campaigns—simply order a new print to update the message. For companies attending numerous events, the mobility of the counter is also crucial. A lightweight construction and ease of transport make moving from place to place significantly easier. It is also worth considering additional features, such as the availability of shelves for storing promotional materials.

Exhibition counter for tastings and promotions

Exhibition counters play a significant role in generating interest in the brand among potential customers. Promotional and tasting events are a great opportunity to showcase the company's offerings, including new products and services. Placed in strategic locations, such as main aisles in a store or near checkout counters, the counter not only grabs attention but also invites interaction with the offer. Tastings give customers the chance to directly experience product quality, which often leads to quicker purchasing decisions. Moreover, free samples build trust in the brand and leave a lasting positive impression. The counter also has convenient shelves for storing promotional materials, making organization easier and facilitating the work of promoters.

Advertising counter for various events

Counters can be used in many roles, from information points to tasting tables and event receptions. Their flexibility and quick graphic customization options allow the counter to be adapted to the character of any event. Eye-catching graphics and a well-thought-out design can attract attention even in a crowded setting, which is crucial for effective promotion. Regardless of the location and nature of the event, advertising counters offer invaluable support in building a positive brand image.

What materials do we use in our counters?

In our counters, we use only the highest quality materials, which ensure durability, aesthetics, and functionality. Aluminum, furniture boards, polyester fabrics, and PVC are the primary components of our products, selected for their durability, resistance to mechanical damage, and elegant appearance. Sublimation and solvent printing, used for producing prints, provide vivid graphics with excellent color reproduction. The use of eco-friendly production methods, such as water-based printing, allows us to create environmentally friendly products, which is increasingly appreciated by our customers. As a result, our counters not only look great but also meet the highest quality and ecological standards.

What exhibition counters can you find in adsystem's offer?

In adsystem's offer, you will find a wide selection of counters tailored to various needs and events. We offer both elegant models for formal exhibitions and lightweight, easy-to-assemble solutions perfect for dynamic events. For companies looking for modern tools, we offer counters with backlighting, which increase booth visibility, as well as models with a pneumatic system that allows the product to be set up in seconds.

Classic counters

Built from durable aluminum and solid furniture boards, classic counters are reliable advertising carriers. Prints for these models are made on laminated PVC boards. They are mounted on the structure using strong Velcro, enabling quick and easy graphic replacement. Customization options—from logos to graphics—allow for effective brand promotion at any event.

Textile counters

Our textile counters are a modern solution that works in various situations—from trade shows to social campaigns. The textile material used in production is a polyester fabric with added stretch, which perfectly fits the structure, does not wrinkle, and is easy to store. The sublimation printing technique used ensures vibrant and long-lasting colors that do not fade even with intensive use. The lightweight construction and ease of transport make the advertising counter extremely convenient, especially for frequent participation in events. These counters are suitable for conferences, educational fairs, and social campaigns where mobility, aesthetics, and practicality are essential.

LED counters

LED counters are a modern solution for companies looking to stand out from the competition. Thanks to built-in LED lights, the graphics printed on textiles are backlit and attract attention from a greater distance, which is particularly useful at large industry events. Importantly, the materials we use guarantee even light diffusion and complete illumination of the advertising message.

Tasting stands

Tasting stands are promotional tools designed for presenting food products in supermarkets, festivals, and trade shows. They provide a convenient space for serving free samples, which effectively draws customer attention and encourages purchases. The stands are equipped with functional shelves for storing promotional materials, and their compact construction allows for easy portability and quick setup in various locations. Thanks to high-quality prints and a modern look, tasting stands not only catch the eye but also build positive associations with the brand, which is crucial in the customer's decision-making process.

Pop-Up counters

Pop-up counters are a modern and practical solution for companies needing mobile and easy-to-assemble promotional tools. To fully set up the counter and make it ready for use, just one smooth movement is enough. This functionality is particularly valuable during dynamic events where time is crucial. Thanks to the pop-up system, the product is ready for action in seconds, without the need for tools or complex assembly. Additionally, the counter is equipped with LED lighting that evenly illuminates the graphics and makes the booth stand out from afar. Combining quick setup and impressive lighting, pop-up counters become an indispensable tool for companies looking to highlight their offerings in any situation.

How do advertising counters help increase company visibility?

By choosing advertising counters, you invest in a tool that not only enhances your brand's prestige but also effectively increases its visibility. With a modern design and full customization options, the product draws attention and directs visitors' focus to your offer. As a result, counters become the heart of every event, and your company gains a better position for acquiring potential customers. Moreover, a professional presentation builds trust and strengthens the brand image. By creating a central point that engages and invites interaction, advertising counters make your company more visible, better remembered, and more often chosen by customers.
