The Double DTF event set is a ready-to-use solution that allows you to create a spacious and professional stand for any event. The set includes two large adFrame DTF textile frames and two backlit adTribune Expo counters. The double configuration enables the organization of a large exhibition space, increasing your brands visibility and enhancing customer interaction.

Why choose the Double DTF event set?

With its large advertising space and dual contact points, this set allows you to fully maximize the potential of any event, providing space for both presentations and customer interactions.

Optimal use of space

The Double DTF set allows for the arrangement of an "L"-shaped stand. This layout offers a large advertising area and convenient spots for customer conversations. With two counters, you can organize two separate contact points, increasing customer service efficiency and boosting your brands promotion.

Large display space for dynamic events

Two 400 x 250 cm adFrame DTF frames offer extensive advertising space, ensuring that all content is visible from a distance. The double-sided display helps your messages reach a broader audience, which is crucial at high-traffic events. Additionally, the elegant adTribune Expo counters with LED backlighting effectively catch the eye, even in dimly lit areas, making your brand stand out and encouraging participant engagement.

Intuitive assembly and high quality

Both the adFrame DTF frames and the adTribune Expo counters are designed for easy assembly and user convenience. They can be set up without tools. The durable, high-quality materials guarantee longevity and a polished appearance over time. Moreover, interchangeable graphics allow for quick adaptation of the stand to different campaigns – simply order new prints to refresh your promotional message.
