Battery-powered illuminated promotional counter — up to 24h of operation

adTribune Pop-up Battery is an illuminated promotional counter powered by a battery. It can shine for up to 24 hours, which means two full trade show days without charging and without connecting the counter to power. You can place it anywhere on your stand or in the exhibition hall — in the middle of the space, near the entrance, in a communication aisle or in the area where you talk to customers — and stop worrying about access to a socket.

The battery is included in the set, and you can check its level on the percentage display placed at the bottom of the structure, next to the counter feet. A full charge takes around 5 hours.

The pop-up system also makes assembly faster and easier. Just lift the folded structure, attach the support and the print, and the counter is ready to use. The tabletop also features a built-in wireless charger, so you can charge your phone during the event.

Advantages
More freedom when setting up your stand
Battery power gives you much more freedom when planning the space. The counter does not have to stand next to a wall, an extension cord or the nearest power source. You can place it where visitors naturally stop or where your team talks to customers.
Intuitive tool-free assembly
The adTribune Pop-up Battery structure is based on the pop-up system known from other counters in this series. You do not have to assemble it from separate elements — the whole structure opens automatically when you lift the frame up. All you need to add is the support and the print.
Charger always within reach
The tabletop includes a built-in wireless charger that lets you charge your phone during the event. Simply place your smartphone in the marked area on the tabletop to top up the battery while talking to customers, presenting your offer or working at the stand.

adTribune Pop-up Battery — every event detail taken care of

adTribune Pop-up Battery has everything you need for a successful event. It is tool-free, self-sufficient and visible from a distance.

To turn on the counter lighting, simply press the button placed on the side of the frame. Battery power removes the need to connect it to electricity during the event. You can check the charge level on the percentage display at the bottom of the structure, next to the counter feet — so it is easy to see how much energy is left and whether you need to plan charging after the workday is over.

A full charge takes around 5 hours, and after that the counter can shine for up to 24 hours. That gives you a comfortable reserve for a full day of work, and for shorter events — also for the next use.

The sides of the counter are painted white, so they match the battery container and the whole product looks consistent even up close. The textile print can be replaced before your next campaigns, product launches or events, while you keep using the same structure.

The model is available as a complete set with a battery and as a print only.
